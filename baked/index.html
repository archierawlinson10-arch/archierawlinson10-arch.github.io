Baked cafe

An independent cafe at Frankby Stiles, just outside Birkenhead — great coffee, good food and a welcoming atmosphere for the local community. Dogs very much included.

Call 0151 625 1293 Instagram

Simple, elegant, and genuinely tasteful.

Baked opened in March 2021, in a converted corner of Frankby Stiles in the Wirral countryside. What started with eleven tables and forty-four seats has since grown into a larger cafe, with 28 tables, seating for 120 inside and additional outdoor seating.
